# Audit: OpenAI's "ETH is false" claim

## The claim

> Surprisingly, our system has recently proved that the ETH is false, a result that was subsequently verified in Lean.

**Source:** [OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities)
**Who:** OpenAI, in its announcement "Working with academic communities to build scientific understanding"

## Steelman

The strongest fair reading is that OpenAI is not asking readers to take a press release on trust. On this reading, an internal model produced a deterministic algorithm that counts satisfying assignments for k-CNF formulas in subexponential time, which would break the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH) as usually stated, and that algorithm was formalized in Lean, a proof assistant that checks each logical step mechanically. The paper and the Lean proof are public, the original proposers of ETH are organizing an open examination, and OpenAI itself frames the manuscripts as raw research data rather than finished science. If the formal statement in Lean matches the conjecture mathematicians mean by ETH, and if the examination confirms it, then the sentence is literally true and the result is a landmark.

## What was demonstrated

- **OpenAI published the ETH paper and its Lean formalization on October 26, 2026.** OpenAI says its AI found the natural language disproof on October 16, 2026 and that the result was formalized in Lean on October 17, 2026, and that both the writeup and the Lean proof are in its public GitHub repository. ([OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities); [GitHub](https://github.com/openai/math))
- **Anthropic posted its own ETH refutation hours later, dated to the same day.** Anthropic's paper, credited to "Claude, Anthropic," with a Lean 4 formalization, appeared on GitHub at 03:07 UTC on October 27, 2026. Both companies say their models found the result on October 16. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained))
- **The review is led by the people who proposed ETH, under stated independence terms.** Russell Impagliazzo and Ramamohan Paturi of UC San Diego, with MIT's Ryan Williams, are shepherding the examination. OpenAI says participants sign no nondisclosure agreements, are unpaid, and speak only for themselves, and that it agreed to make all raw data public and to fund a conference on the work. ([OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities); [Dealroom, October 27, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/167089-openai-says-its-ai-disproved-a-core-computer-science-assumption-gives-5m/))
- **Both papers disclaim the two readings that traveled furthest.** Refuting ETH does not settle P versus NP, because subexponential time is not polynomial time, and neither paper yields practical algorithms for NP-complete problems. Anthropic's paper says its result is purely asymptotic and says nothing about solving formulas in practice. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained); [WebProNews, October 27, 2026](https://www.webpronews.com/openai-anthropic-exponential-time-hypothesis-refuted-sat/))
- **OpenAI committed money and process around the review, not just a headline.** Its announcement points to a $5 million contribution to the Simons Foundation for workshops, conferences, and programs to help researchers understand and build on AI-produced results, and says it will ensure funding for researchers to examine these results independently. ([OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities))

## What was claimed but not shown

- **No independent human confirmation existed as of October 27, 2026.** Papex, after checking its sources on October 27, states that none of the three results OpenAI announced has been independently confirmed yet. WebProNews likewise reports that neither ETH paper has been peer reviewed and no outside group has confirmed either proof yet. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained); [WebProNews, October 27, 2026](https://www.webpronews.com/openai-anthropic-exponential-time-hypothesis-refuted-sat/))
- **"Verified in Lean" is not the same as verified by the field.** Lean checks that the formal statements follow from the axioms. It does not check that the formal statements are the statements mathematicians mean by ETH. Anthropic's own repository states the caveat plainly: "Lean checks the proofs, but no machine checks that this file says what the paper says." ([WebProNews, October 27, 2026](https://www.webpronews.com/openai-anthropic-exponential-time-hypothesis-refuted-sat/); [Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained))
- **The machine-checked bound is weaker than the paper headline.** OpenAI's Lean scope note gives an exponent of n(log log n) squared over the square root of log n, weaker than the paper's n log log n over the square root of log n. Both bounds are subexponential, so this gap does not defeat the ETH claim on its own, but the checked statement and the headline statement are not word for word the same. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained))
- **The examiners explicitly refuse the endorsement the headlines implied.** Impagliazzo, Paturi, and Williams wrote: "Our involvement should not be taken as certification of the claim. A result of this significance requires careful, independent scrutiny." Their participation is a review arrangement, not a verdict. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained); [Dealroom, October 27, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/167089-openai-says-its-ai-disproved-a-core-computer-science-assumption-gives-5m/))
- **The same-day discovery story rests only on the two companies' own statements.** Both date the discovery to October 16, but the first public files are the GitHub releases of October 26 and 27. No independent timestamp from October 16 has been published, so the public record cannot yet settle who found what first, or whether the two routes are genuinely independent. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained))
- **The model, prompts, attempts, and compute behind the result were not published.** OpenAI describes "our system" and "our internal models" without naming the model that produced the proof. That leaves the provenance part of the claim, as distinct from the proof itself, uncheckable from the public record. ([OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities); [The AI Pin, October 27, 2026](https://theaipin.com/science/openai-claims-three-discoveries-in-math-cs-and-physics))

## Receipts

- OpenAI's original announcement, with the quoted ETH sentence, the October 16 and 17 dates, the shepherd group, and the public GitHub release. ([OpenAI, October 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities))
- Side by side comparison of the OpenAI and Anthropic ETH papers, their Lean formalizations, the weaker Lean exponent, the open letter from the examiners, and the statement that none of the three results has been independently confirmed yet. ([Papex, October 27, 2026](https://papex.org/blog/claimed-verified-explained))
- Neither paper peer reviewed, no outside confirmation yet, the Lean caveat about what the file says versus what the paper says, and the practical irrelevance of the asymptotic bound. ([WebProNews, October 27, 2026](https://www.webpronews.com/openai-anthropic-exponential-time-hypothesis-refuted-sat/))
- The $5 million Simons Foundation contribution, the unpaid and no-NDA review terms, and the examiners' statement that their involvement is not an endorsement. ([Dealroom, October 27, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/167089-openai-says-its-ai-disproved-a-core-computer-science-assumption-gives-5m/))
- The public ETH paper and Lean proof in OpenAI's repository. ([GitHub](https://github.com/openai/math))
- What could not be verified as of publication: whether any of the three proofs is correct, whether the Lean formalizations capture the full conjectures, and which internal model produced the proofs. ([The AI Pin, October 27, 2026](https://theaipin.com/science/openai-claims-three-discoveries-in-math-cs-and-physics))
- Anthropic's parallel claim: a Claude paper refuting ETH, found October 16 with no human input according to Anthropic, with a Lean 4 proof, and a bound that only beats brute force at enormous problem sizes. ([CellCog, October 27, 2026](https://cellcog.ai/blog/claude-refutes-eth-3-sat/))

## Verdict

**Unverifiable**

Two labs publishing Lean formalizations of the same refutation, dated to the same day, makes this a serious candidate result rather than a bare press release, and the review arrangement is genuinely open. But as of October 28, no independent human examination has confirmed either proof, no paper has been peer reviewed, and the decisive check, whether the formal statements match the conjecture itself, is still open by the examiners' own account. That is a verdict about the state of the evidence today, not about the eventual truth of the claim, and it should be revisited when the examination reports.

## Why it matters

If the refutation holds, hundreds of papers that derive conditional hardness results from ETH lose their footing, even though P versus NP stays open, cryptography at standard key sizes is not shown broken, and no SAT solver gets faster. That combination, a huge theoretical consequence with no practical consequence, is exactly why the wording matters: "verified in Lean" sounds like the science is settled, when the settled part is only that a formal argument checks out mechanically. The more durable story is the process OpenAI is testing, with outside shepherds, public raw data, and no academic credit claimed for the models, and whether that process can turn AI-generated candidate proofs into understanding the field can trust. Until the examiners report, the honest label for ETH is claimed, not resolved.
