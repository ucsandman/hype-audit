# Audit: Alibaba Qwen4-Max "achieves top rankings across leading benchmarks"

## The claim

> Qwen4-Max achieves top rankings across leading benchmarks, particularly in coding, agentic tasks and visual reasoning.

**Source:** [Alibaba Unveils Qwen4-Max: New AI Teammate for Work, Creativity, and Discovery, November 16, 2026](https://www.alibabacloud.com/blog/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max-new-ai-teammate-for-work-creativity-and-discovery_603783)
**Who:** Alibaba Cloud, in its Qwen4-Max launch announcement; the same release says the model "ranked 6th on Code Arena, a real-world coding leaderboard"

## Steelman

The strongest fair reading is that Alibaba has shipped a genuinely strong new flagship that is dramatically cheaper than its Western rivals, and that its coding and agentic results justify the headline. On this reading, Qwen4-Max is a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 71 billion active parameters per token, native vision, and a one-million-token context window, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, roughly a tenth of the list price of the leading Western frontier models. Alibaba's launch table shows it beating Claude Opus 5.5 on DeepSWE v1.1 (75.7 to 74.1), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (48.5 to 44.2), and Agents' Last Exam v1.1 (46.7 to 41.5), while its proprietary Work360 evaluation shows it outperforming its predecessor while cutting the average cost per task by around 60 percent. If that reading holds, the launch is a real capability step and a pricing event at the same time, and "top rankings" is a reasonable summary of a model that leads or nearly leads the coding and agentic boards that matter.

## What was demonstrated

- **Qwen4-Max leads on several vendor-reported coding and agentic tests.** On Alibaba's launch chart it scores 75.7 on DeepSWE v1.1 against 74.1 for Claude Opus 5.5 and 71.2 for Kimi K3.1 Pro, 48.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 44.2 for Opus 5.5, and 46.7 on Agents' Last Exam v1.1 against 41.5 for Opus 5.5. ([AI in China, November 2026](https://www.ainchina.com/blog/qwen4-max-five-day-sprint-china-cheapest-frontier-2026/); [TradingView, November 2026](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cryptobriefing:2a230d8c1094b:0-alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max-claims-it-beats-rival-models-on-coding/))
- **The price is real and dramatically lower than the Western frontier.** Alibaba lists $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, and Artificial Analysis independently measured $3.27 per Intelligence Index task, 67 percent less than the $10.02 for Qwen3.8-Max and below the $3.68 for Kimi K3.1 Pro. ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen4-max); [Intelligent Living, November 2026](https://www.intelligentliving.co/qwen4-max-alibaba-agent-tested/))
- **It is a real improvement over its predecessor on every test where both were measured.** Alibaba's chart shows Qwen4-Max beating Qwen3.8-Max on all sixteen shared benchmarks, and Artificial Analysis scores it 43 on its Intelligence Index against 34 for Qwen3.8-Max. ([AI in China, November 2026](https://www.ainchina.com/blog/qwen4-max-five-day-sprint-china-cheapest-frontier-2026/); [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen4-max))
- **Its Code Arena standing is genuinely strong, if not first.** Alibaba's own release reports 6th on Code Arena at launch, and LMArena's WebDev board showed Qwen4-Max (xHigh) at 6th with a score of 1,750 in the days after launch, with category ranks of 4th in Gaming, 6th in Frontend and Brand and Marketing, and 7th in React as of the November 23 update. ([Alibaba Cloud, November 16, 2026](https://www.alibabacloud.com/blog/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max-new-ai-teammate-for-work-creativity-and-discovery_603783); [LMArena Code Arena WebDev](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev/models/qwen4-max-xhigh-lm5r))

## What was claimed but not shown

- **"Top rankings across leading benchmarks" does not survive the independent composites.** Artificial Analysis scores Qwen4-Max at 43 on its Intelligence Index, ranking it 38th of 81 models and below the median of 44 for models in a similar price tier. The same index puts Claude Opus 5.5 at 59, Gemini 4 Carbon at 60, Claude Fable 5.2 at 65, and the open-weight Kimi K3.1 Pro at 46, three points ahead of Qwen4-Max. ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen4-max); [Datastudios, November 2026](https://www.datastudios.org/post/qwen4-max-vs-qwen3-8-max-comparison); [Invidelabs, November 2026](https://blog.invidelabs.com/qwen4-max-benchmarks-pricing/))
- **Other independent boards place it in the middle of the pack, not at the top.** It ranks 14th on LMArena's Agent Arena leaderboard, 13th of 41 on the Vals.ai Index at 63.20 percent accuracy, and 42nd of 218 on the BenchLM public leaderboard. ([BeInCrypto, November 2026](https://beincrypto.com/alibaba-qwen4-max-stock-impact/); [AI in China, November 2026](https://www.ainchina.com/blog/qwen4-max-five-day-sprint-china-cheapest-frontier-2026/))
- **Even on Alibaba's own chosen sixteen benchmarks, it does not top the chart.** Qwen4-Max leads on 4 of the 16, while Opus 5.5 leads on 7, and it trails Opus 5.5 on Humanity's Last Exam (51.7 to 56.3) and on the vendor-reported CodeArena Elo (1750 to 1816 for Opus 5.5 in the full chart). ([Gabs, November 2026](https://www.gabs.app/blog/qwen4-max-release-open-weights); [Intelligent Living, November 2026](https://www.intelligentliving.co/qwen4-max-alibaba-agent-tested/))
- **The headline's strongest supporting number comes from a benchmark Alibaba built for this launch.** Work360 is Alibaba's proprietary evaluation, built specifically for this release, and it compares Qwen4-Max only against its predecessor, not against rival models. It is useful evidence about Alibaba's own progress and costs, and weak evidence for a cross-vendor "top rankings" claim. ([AI in China, November 2026](https://www.ainchina.com/blog/qwen4-max-five-day-sprint-china-cheapest-frontier-2026/))
- **The launch quietly breaks with the open-weights tradition the Qwen name was built on.** Qwen4-Max ships API-only with no weights released and no date given for one, while Alibaba promises only that smaller Qwen4 models will be open-sourced. That is a separate fact from the benchmark claim, but it means the "top rankings" cannot be independently reproduced by anyone outside Alibaba's API. ([AI in China, November 2026](https://www.ainchina.com/blog/qwen4-max-five-day-sprint-china-cheapest-frontier-2026/); [Gabs, November 2026](https://www.gabs.app/blog/qwen4-max-release-open-weights))
- **LMArena's own board moved against the launch-week framing within days.** The overall WebDev ranking that Alibaba cited as 6th stood at 9th, with a score of 1,752, by the November 23 update, which is a reminder that a single leaderboard snapshot is a weak anchor for a "top rankings" headline. ([LMArena Code Arena WebDev](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev/models/qwen4-max-xhigh-lm5r))

## Verdict

**Overstated**

Something real sits underneath: Qwen4-Max is a genuinely strong coding and agentic model, a clear step up from Qwen3.8-Max on every shared test, and its pricing undercuts the Western frontier by roughly an order of magnitude on list rates. But "top rankings across leading benchmarks" runs ahead of the evidence. The independent composites that weigh many evaluations at once put it 38th of 81 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 14th on Agent Arena, and 42nd of 218 on BenchLM, behind Opus 5.5 by 16 points on the index and behind the open-weight Kimi K3.1 Pro by 3. Even Alibaba's own release concedes 6th on Code Arena in the same paragraph as the claim, and its own sixteen-benchmark chart gives Opus 5.5 more outright wins. A fairer headline is that Alibaba shipped a strong, cheap coding model that ranks in the top ten on web development boards and mid-pack on general intelligence composites.

## Why it matters

The gap between a vendor's chart and an independent composite is now the single most reliable signal in model launches, and Qwen4-Max is a clean example of why. Alibaba's numbers are not fabricated: the model really does beat Opus 5.5 on DeepSWE and Terminal-Bench, and the price really is a market event. But a launch headline that says "top rankings" invites readers to believe the model sits at the top of the field, and the independent evidence says it sits in the upper middle, with the gap widest on the broadest tests. The distinction matters most for buyers, who will see the headline, check one leaderboard, and sign a contract on the basis of a ranking that three other boards contradict. Price is the honest story here, and it did not need the ranking claim to be one.
