## The claim

> Harvey is "quickly becoming an example of how an entire industry, law, can both heavily use AI and not rely on proprietary frontier AI labs like OpenAI and Anthropic."

**Source:** [TechCrunch, September 9, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/09/harvey-hits-15-5b-valuation-months-after-reaching-11b/)
**Who:** TechCrunch's framing of Harvey's $550M raise at a $15.5B valuation and its first in-house model, Harvey Tenet

## Steelman

This is a real data point for a real trend. Harvey Tenet was built from the open-weight Kimi K3 and post-trained on legal data with inference provider Fireworks, which also helped Cursor train its homegrown model. A $15.5B legal AI company shipping its own model on open weights is genuinely notable, and it fits the broader pattern of industries reducing dependence on any single proprietary lab.

## What was demonstrated

- Harvey raised $550M at a $15.5B valuation, its third valuation jump in about nine months (TechCrunch).
- Harvey Tenet exists: built from open-weight Kimi K3, post-trained with legal data with help from Fireworks (TechCrunch).

## What was claimed but not shown

- One company's one model does not show that an entire industry no longer relies on frontier labs. Harvey's own product history and the broader legal AI market still run substantially on frontier models.
- "Not rely on" does heavy lifting: building on Kimi K3, itself a frontier-class open model from a major lab, is a different supply chain, not independence from frontier AI.

## Receipts

- TechCrunch on the raise and the model: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/09/harvey-hits-15-5b-valuation-months-after-reaching-11b/

## Verdict

**Overstated**

The model is real and the open-weights trend is real, but one post-trained model at one company does not demonstrate industry-wide independence from frontier labs. The claim generalizes a data point into a movement.

## Why it matters

"Open weights means independence" is becoming a reflexive take. The actual story, supply-chain diversification rather than independence, matters more for anyone betting a product roadmap on it.
