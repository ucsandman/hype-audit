# Audit: Isomorphic Labs "positive preclinical results" and a $5B round with no drug in humans yet

## The claim

> This momentum is yielding positive preclinical results, both for internal programs in oncology and immunology and with partners, setting the stage for clinical entry.

**Source:** [Isomorphic Labs announces $5 Billion Series C funding, November 17, 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/isomorphiclabs-website-public-artifacts/ISOMORPHIC_LABS_FUNDING_17-11-26.pdf)
**Who:** Isomorphic Labs, in its Series C funding announcement; CEO Demis Hassabis added in the same release that "our drug design engine is generating molecules with unprecedented precision and speed"

## Steelman

The strongest fair reading is that a serious, well funded drug design effort is working as designed and is now close to the clinic. On this reading, Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet company spun out of Google DeepMind in 2021 and led by Nobel laureate Demis Hassabis, has spent five years building IsoDDE, a drug design engine that designs, predicts, and refines molecules with AI. It has real pharmaceutical partners in Novartis, Eli Lilly, and Johnson and Johnson, it reported in February 2026 that IsoDDE more than doubled AlphaFold 3 accuracy on a difficult protein-drug prediction benchmark, and it has now raised $5 billion, one of the largest private financings ever, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, to scale the engine and build the clinical infrastructure that takes candidates into human trials. If that reading holds, the announcement marks the moment AI-designed medicines stop being a research demo and start becoming drugs.

## What was demonstrated

- **The $5 billion Series C is real and confirmed.** Isomorphic announced the round on November 17, 2026, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz joining as a new investor and Alphabet investing further, alongside all existing investors. Reuters independently reported the round the same day. ([Isomorphic Labs, November 17, 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/isomorphiclabs-website-public-artifacts/ISOMORPHIC_LABS_FUNDING_17-11-26.pdf); [Reuters, November 17, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ai-drug-developer-isomorphic-raises-5-billion-series-c-round-2026-11-17/))
- **The company has genuine pharmaceutical partnerships.** Reuters reports that Isomorphic lists Novartis, Eli Lilly, and Johnson and Johnson as partners, and the company signed an agreement with Johnson and Johnson in January 2026 to investigate small-molecule drugs and larger biological treatments. ([Reuters, November 17, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ai-drug-developer-isomorphic-raises-5-billion-series-c-round-2026-11-17/); [The Neuron, November 3, 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-design-human-trials/))
- **IsoDDE has reported benchmark wins, and one designed molecule showed activity in the lab.** In February 2026 the company reported that IsoDDE more than doubled AlphaFold 3 accuracy on a difficult protein-drug prediction benchmark, and in September it described an automated drug design experiment in which scientists made selected compounds and tested them in the laboratory, with one candidate showing activity against its intended biological target. Both results are company-reported. ([The Neuron, November 3, 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-design-human-trials/))
- **The money is explicitly earmarked for the clinic, not only for models.** The release says proceeds will support building scalable AI-first translational and clinical development infrastructure as the company advances its drug candidates to clinical trials. ([Isomorphic Labs, November 17, 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/isomorphiclabs-website-public-artifacts/ISOMORPHIC_LABS_FUNDING_17-11-26.pdf))
- **The underlying science has a real peer-reviewed anchor.** AlphaFold 3, co-developed with Google DeepMind, was peer-reviewed in Nature. That is the foundation the company builds on, and it is the one headline result that does not depend on the company's word alone. ([Healthcare Discovery, November 16, 2026](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-discovery/))

## What was claimed but not shown

- **"Positive preclinical results" comes with no data, no named candidate, and no named target.** The release names no molecule, no target, no program count, no trial phase, and no date, and it includes no preclinical data. As of November 16, 2026, no trial registration and no first dosed patient had been found, and the company had not named its drug candidates or published preclinical data. ([TechTimes, November 17, 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330909/20261117/isomorphic-labs-raises-5-billion-discloses-no-human-trial-ai-drug-benchmarks-are-not-patient-data.htm); [Healthcare Discovery, November 16, 2026](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-discovery/))
- **"Unprecedented precision and speed" is asserted, not measured.** Hassabis's line in the release gives no baseline, no metric, and no comparison, so there is nothing an outside reader can check it against. The February benchmark claim is also company-reported, with no independent reproduction cited. ([Isomorphic Labs, November 17, 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/isomorphiclabs-website-public-artifacts/ISOMORPHIC_LABS_FUNDING_17-11-26.pdf); [The Neuron, November 3, 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-design-human-trials/))
- **The clinical entry the claim promises has already slipped once, and the date is now unconfirmed.** Hassabis said in January at Davos that first clinical trials would begin by the end of 2026, after earlier pointing to the end of 2025. Dealroom reports he would not give Bloomberg an updated target, saying only that the company is on track with its regulatory submissions and will have more to announce in 2027. The Series C release gives no trial date. ([TechTimes, November 17, 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330909/20261117/isomorphic-labs-raises-5-billion-discloses-no-human-trial-ai-drug-benchmarks-are-not-patient-data.htm); [Dealroom, November 17, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/173568-isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-to-push-ai-designed-drugs-toward-trials/))
- **The company's own president describes the programs as still deep in preclinical work.** At Bloomberg Tech in London on November 2, president Max Jaderberg described the company as still in its first innings, with programs deep in preclinical work, and predicted AI would eventually design all medicines. That is a statement about the future, not a result. ([The Neuron, November 3, 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/isomorphic-labs-ai-drug-design-human-trials/))
- **The $50 billion valuation attached to the round in coverage is unconfirmed.** Isomorphic declined to share its valuation, Reuters reported. Bloomberg reported on November 4 that the talks valued the company at about $50 billion, citing people familiar with the matter, and TechCrunch and Dealroom both note the figure comes from that reporting, not from the company. Neither the Series B in May nor the first outside round in March 2025 came with a disclosed valuation either. ([Reuters, November 17, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ai-drug-developer-isomorphic-raises-5-billion-series-c-round-2026-11-17/); [TechCrunch, November 17, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-as-it-looks-to-take-drug-candidates-to-clinical-trials/); [Dealroom, November 17, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/173568-isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-to-push-ai-designed-drugs-toward-trials/))
- **No regulator has yet approved an AI-designed drug.** That is the standard the "setting the stage for clinical entry" framing has to meet, and it has not been met by any company. The first human data from an Isomorphic-designed molecule is still the test that matters, and it does not exist yet. ([Dealroom, November 17, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/173568-isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-to-push-ai-designed-drugs-toward-trials/))

## Verdict

**Overstated**

Something real sits underneath: $5 billion in confirmed funding, real partnerships with three major drug companies, a peer-reviewed foundation in AlphaFold 3, and company-reported benchmark and lab results that point in the right direction. But the headline claim runs ahead of the evidence. "Positive preclinical results" is asserted with no published data, no named candidate, and no named target; "unprecedented precision and speed" has no metric attached; no Isomorphic-designed molecule has been tested in a person; the first-trial target has already slipped from the end of 2025 to the end of 2026 and is now unconfirmed; and the $50 billion valuation in coverage is a Bloomberg-reported figure the company has never confirmed. A fairer headline is that a serious, well funded preclinical company raised a record round to build the infrastructure for trials it has not yet started.

## Why it matters

A $5 billion round at a reported $50 billion valuation prices a company with no drug in humans as one of the most valuable private biotechs in the world, and that price will be quoted as proof that AI drug design works. It is not proof. It is a bet by sophisticated investors on a team, a platform, and a set of unpublished results, and bets are not evidence. The distinction matters because the real test is narrow and near: whether an Isomorphic-designed candidate enters a human trial by the end of 2026, the target Hassabis set in January. If it does, the preclinical claims start to look like a fair preview. If it slips again, the gap between the funding narrative and the clinical reality is the story. Either way, the verdict will be written by a trial registration, not by a funding announcement.
